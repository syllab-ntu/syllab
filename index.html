SyLLab · NTU Singapore

Symmetric Cryptography and Machine Learning Lab

We design and analyze ciphers (mainly symmetric-key), study the many possible links between cryptography and AI, and build AI models whose decisions can be explained and verified.

Research topics/highlights

Cryptography

Primitives design

We design cryptographic primitives, including permutations, ciphers, and modes of operation, for a wide range of practical uses.

GIFT, LED, SKINNY and Deoxys-TBC (ISO/IEC 18033-7), PHOTON (ISO/IEC 29192-5), and Deoxys-II (CAESAR first choice for defense in depth).

Implementations

We build and evaluate fast, small, and secure implementations of cryptographic algorithms in software and hardware.

Fixslicing · ARM Cortex-M speed record RustCrypto’s portable AES Bit-Sliding LIGHTER Side-channel analysis

Cryptanalysis

We cryptanalyse both long-established and recent symmetric-key primitives.

SHA-1 collisions Boomerang connectivity table (BCT) Internal differential attack Super-Sbox cryptanalysis

Automated methods and AI-assisted cryptanalysis

We use constraint solvers to search for patterns in ciphers and we study how neural distinguishers can be used in cryptanalysis.

Open Cryptanalysis Platform Understanding neural distinguishers

AI

AI security

We study neural-network weight recovery, adversarial attacks and the limits of model confidentiality.

Deep networks CNNs with max-pooling

Interpretable AI

We build AI models whose predictions can be traced through truth tables and rules that people can inspect.

TTnet TT-rules TT-Sparse

Compact AI

We study how to make AI models as compact as possible for a given task, with a focus on fast inference, resource-constrained environments, and hardware implementations on FPGAs and ASICs.

Beyond Filter Pruning

Privacy-preserving AI

We study how to design AI models that run efficiently with privacy-preserving technologies such as fully homomorphic encryption (FHE).

TT-TFHE Implicant
